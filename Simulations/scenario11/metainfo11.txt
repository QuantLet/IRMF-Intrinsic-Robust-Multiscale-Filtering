Description: Simulations for IRMF.  We consider two choices of signal, two types of errors different parameters. 

Signal1 (c=1 ): X_t = 1.2sin(2\pi t) + 0.5sin(6\pi t)  for t \in [0,1]

Error1 (t=1): Y_t=X_t with probabilities 1-p and Y_t=0 with probabilities p. 
                      If t=1&tc=1, p=0.2. If t=1&tc=2, p=0.4

In each simulation, we generate n observations. Conduct the kth component IRMF estimate \tilde S_t^{(k)}. We also repeat the simulation m times and calculate the kth component (population level) IRMF estimate S_t^{(k)}. 

We compare the estimated error \tilde S_t^{(k)}- S_t^{(k)} and deviation from signal \sum_{l=1}^k\tilde S_t^{(l)}-X_t. Provide pointwise QQplot, sup estimated error plot, sup deviation from signal plot, scaled integrated mean square of estimated error plot and scaled integrated mean square of deviation from signal plot.
